1. Service Scope: Transfer Pipeline vs. Privora Drive

Privora operates two distinct storage models:

  • Ephemeral Transfer Pipeline: Disposable, time-limited file handoffs subject to auto-expiration (1h–72h) or instant "Burn-on-Read" shredding.
  • Privora Drive: Encrypted cloud storage preserved indefinitely in the user's private vault until intentionally deleted.

2. Storage Allocations & Quotas

Each account is bound to designated storage ceilings:

  • Free Tier: Includes 5.00 GB of permanent Privora Drive storage and up to 2.00 GB per single ephemeral transfer.
  • Privora Pro ($4/mo): Expands capacity to 200.00 GB of permanent Privora Drive storage and up to 50.00 GB per single transfer.

Attempts to exceed assigned quotas will be rejected at the API boundary until storage is freed or an upgraded subscription is activated.

3. Acceptable Use Policy

You agree not to store or distribute through Privora or Privora Drive:

  • Unauthorized copyrighted media, pirated cinema, or software cracking archives (DMCA violations).
  • Malware, exploits, viruses, ransomware, or malicious bot payloads.
  • Content that violates international laws or privacy regulations.
  • Automated abuse designed to circumvent storage limits or throttle server nodes.

4. Deletion & Data Retention Disclaimers

When a user initiates the "Delete" action on Privora Drive, the file object is immediately and irreversibly shredded from Cloudflare R2 edge servers. Privora does not retain secondary backup copies of deleted files. Users are solely responsible for maintaining local copies of critical documents.

5. Subscriptions & Billing

Privora Pro is billed monthly at $4.00 USD. Users may cancel renewals anytime via dashboard settings. If a Pro account downgrades to Free while holding in excess of 5 GB of data, upload privileges are paused until total storage is brought within the 5 GB free threshold.

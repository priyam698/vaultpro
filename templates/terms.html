1. Service Scope: Transfer Pipeline vs. Zephyr Drive

Zephyr operates two distinct storage models:

  • Ephemeral Transfer Pipeline: Disposable, time-limited file handoffs subject to auto-expiration (1h–72h) or instant "Burn-on-Read" shredding.
  • Zephyr Drive: Encrypted cloud storage preserved indefinitely in the user's private vault until intentionally deleted.

2. Storage Allocations & Quotas

Each account is bound to designated storage ceilings:

  • Free Starter: Includes 5.00 GB of permanent Zephyr Drive storage and up to 2.00 GB per single ephemeral transfer.
  • Zephyr Micro: Provides 15.00 GB of permanent Drive storage and up to 5.00 GB per single transfer.
  • Zephyr Lite: Provides 30.00 GB of permanent Drive storage and up to 10.00 GB per single transfer.
  • Zephyr Plus: Provides 80.00 GB of permanent Drive storage and up to 25.00 GB per single transfer.
  • Zephyr Pro: Expands capacity to 200.00 GB of permanent Zephyr Drive storage and up to 50.00 GB per single transfer.

Attempts to exceed assigned quotas will be rejected at the API boundary until storage is freed or an upgraded subscription is activated.

3. Acceptable Use Policy

You agree not to store or distribute through Zephyr or Zephyr Drive:

  • Unauthorized copyrighted media, pirated cinema, or software cracking archives (DMCA violations).
  • Malware, exploits, viruses, ransomware, or malicious bot payloads.
  • Content that violates international laws or privacy regulations.
  • Automated abuse designed to circumvent storage limits or throttle server nodes.

4. Deletion & Data Retention Disclaimers

When a user initiates the "Delete" action on Zephyr Drive, the file object is immediately and irreversibly shredded from Cloudflare R2 edge servers. Zephyr does not retain secondary backup copies of deleted files. Users are solely responsible for maintaining local copies of critical documents.

5. Subscriptions & Billing

Zephyr offers tiered subscriptions billed monthly: Micro ($1.80/mo), Lite ($2.50/mo), Plus ($4.50/mo), and Pro ($7.00/mo). Mid-cycle upgrades are seamlessly prorated based on remaining cycle days plus a $0.50 processing fee. If a paid account lapses, a 20-day read-only grace buffer applies before vault pruning down to active tier quotas.

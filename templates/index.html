Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about encrypted transfers, storage quotas, and security protocols.

How does the 4K Ultra HD AI Super-Resolution feature work? ▾

Zephyr Drive Studio integrates Real-ESRGAN neural super-resolution models to upscale raster images, photos, and project graphics up to 4× native resolution (up to 3840×2160 UHD). The model deep-cleans compression artifacts, reconstructs facial contours, and refines edges without blurring. It is available on Zephyr Lite (15 runs/month), Zephyr Plus (60 runs/month), and completely unlimited on Zephyr Pro.

How does the Pay-to-Unlock Escrow service work in groups? ▾

When Pay-to-Unlock is enabled, sharing the link in a group lets each member purchase their own personal access token. Unlocking is strictly individual—one person's purchase will not expose the file to unpaid recipients. Files stream inside a secure viewer protected by dynamic watermarking and anti-screenshot shields.

How do daily E-Sign creation limits work across plans? ▾

Daily E-Sign document creation quotas reset automatically every 24 hours at midnight: Free Starter includes 7 documents/day, Zephyr Micro includes 15 documents/day, Zephyr Lite includes 30 documents/day, Zephyr Plus includes 50 documents/day, and Zephyr Pro provides completely unlimited daily document signing.

What happens to my files if my paid subscription lapses? (20-Day Grace Period) ▾

If your subscription expires, your vault enters an industry-standard 20-day read-only grace period. You can renew or download your files at any time during these 20 days. After 20 days, if your account has not been renewed, your vault will be automatically pruned down to your active tier limit by permanently shredding the oldest files.

Can I upgrade my storage plan in the middle of a billing cycle? ▾

Yes. You can upgrade between Micro (15 GB), Lite (30 GB), Plus (80 GB), and Pro (200 GB) at any time. When upgrading mid-cycle, you are only charged for the prorated price difference based on the remaining days of your billing cycle plus a small $0.50 mid-cycle processing fee.

What is the maximum single transfer file size allowed across tiers? ▾

Single transfer sizes depend on your plan: Free Starter supports up to 2 GB, Zephyr Micro supports 5 GB, Zephyr Lite supports 10 GB, Zephyr Plus supports 25 GB, and Zephyr Pro supports massive uploads up to 50 GB per transfer.

Can Zephyr or third parties view my transferred files? ▾

No. Zephyr operates on a zero-knowledge architecture. When client-side passcodes are enabled, key derivation and payload hashing occur entirely inside your browser. No unencrypted keys or passcodes are ever written to our server logs or databases.

How does the Burn-on-Read (self-shredding) feature work? ▾

When you set the download limit to 1 (Burn on Read 🔥) under Security options, the underlying object stored in our Cloudflare R2 edge pipe is immediately purged the millisecond the recipient finishes their download. The link is permanently destroyed and cannot be accessed again.

🔒 Security Architecture Whitepaper

1. Client-Side Cryptography: All files are hashed and encrypted directly inside your browser using Web Crypto API (AES-256-GCM). Decryption keys reside purely in the URL fragment (`#key=...`) and are never sent to or stored on our servers.

2. Ephemeral Storage Pipe: Data is streamed in chunks directly to Cloudflare R2 edge locations with zero intermediate retention buffers.

3. Burn-on-Read Guarantee: Once activated, completing the first download triggers an immediate unmapping and deletion routine across all storage buckets.

4. Zero-Log Protocol: Server access logs strip IP addresses and file identifiers at the load balancer level.

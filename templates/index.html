Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about encrypted transfers, storage quotas, and security protocols.

Can I use Zephyr Drive to free up space on my phone or laptop? ▾

Yes. You can upload 4K video footage, raw photo galleries, archives, and documents directly to your encrypted Zephyr Drive vault (5 GB Free or 200 GB on Pro). Once uploaded, you can safely delete the local copies from your device.

Can Zephyr or third parties view my transferred files? ▾

No. Zephyr operates on a zero-knowledge architecture. When client-side passcodes are enabled, key derivation and payload hashing occur entirely inside your browser. No unencrypted keys or passcodes are ever written to our server logs or databases.

What is the maximum file transfer size allowed? ▾

The Free Starter tier supports single file packages up to 2.00 GB per transfer. Upgrading to Zephyr Pro ($4/mo) expands your transfer pipeline to 50 GB per single transfer and provides a permanent 200 GB cloud vault.

How does the Burn-on-Read (self-shredding) feature work? ▾

When you set the download limit to 1 (Burn on Read 🔥) under Security options, the underlying object stored in our Cloudflare R2 edge pipe is immediately purged the millisecond the recipient finishes their download. The link is permanently destroyed and cannot be accessed again.

How does sender email verification and OTP security work? ▾

To prevent spoofing and spam abuse, email transfers require a 6-digit verification code sent directly to the address provided in "Your Email". Each OTP is valid for 3 minutes. If an incorrect code is entered 4 consecutive times, that address is placed into a strict 30-minute security lockout.

Do recipients need a Zephyr account to download files? ▾

No. Anyone receiving a Zephyr transfer link or email notification can click and download the files immediately in their web browser without registering an account, installing software, or paying any fees.

Can I upload complete folders with their directory structure intact? ▾

Yes. Click the Add folders button on the transfer dashboard to select an entire directory tree. Zephyr maps your relative file paths to keep nested subfolders organized exactly as they appear on your local hard drive.

How long do transfer links stay active before expiring? ▾

You can select link lifetimes of 1 Hour, 24 Hours, or 3 Days. Pro members can also choose extended retention windows or set transfers to Never Expire to archive them permanently in their Cloud Drive Vault.

What is the Client File Request link? ▾

The Request feature generates a branded drop portal link you can send to clients or collaborators. Anyone with the link can deposit massive video footage or assets directly into your personal Zephyr Vault without needing to create an account.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about encrypted transfers, permanent drive storage, and zero-knowledge privacy.

Can I use Zephyr Drive to free up space on my phone or laptop? ▾

Yes. You can upload 4K video footage, photo galleries, spreadsheets, and archives directly into your encrypted Zephyr Drive vault (5 GB Free or 200 GB on Pro). Once uploaded, you can safely delete the local files from your phone or PC and stream or download them whenever required.

What is the difference between Zephyr Transfer and Zephyr Drive? ▾

Zephyr Transfer is built for disposable handoffs—links automatically expire (1h to 72h) or self-destruct after 1 download. Zephyr Drive is a permanent cloud file manager where your uploaded media stays safely preserved until you intentionally delete it.

Can Zephyr, hosting providers, or third parties view my files? ▾

No. Zephyr operates on zero-knowledge architecture. When passcodes are used, key derivation occurs on your local browser. Zephyr stores encrypted blobs across Cloudflare R2 edge locations without storing your plaintext passwords or keys in our databases.

How does the "Burn on Read" feature work? ▾

When "Max Downloads" is set to 1 (Burn on Read 🔥), the file object is permanently purged from Cloudflare R2 edge servers the exact millisecond the recipient's download stream completes. Any subsequent access displays a permanent expiration notice.

Can I share files stored in my Zephyr Drive with others? ▾

Yes. In your Drive dashboard, clicking the Share Link button next to any stored file copies a direct, secure presigned download link to your clipboard without re-uploading the data.

What happens if I reach the 5 GB Free Drive limit? ▾

Your existing files remain completely secure and downloadable. To upload more files, you can delete old items to free up space or upgrade to Zephyr Pro ($4/mo) for 200 GB of drive storage and 50 GB single uploads.

Are downloads capped or bandwidth-throttled? ▾

No. Because Zephyr is powered by Cloudflare R2's zero-egress edge infrastructure, downloads are never throttled, and transfer recipients do not need an account to download files.

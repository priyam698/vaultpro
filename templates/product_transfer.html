Zephyr Transfer.
Zero-Knowledge Client Delivery.

Built for high-resolution video creators, independent VFX artists, and privacy-conscious teams needing to deliver large master assets without data harvesting, link throttling, or compression.

Files are encrypted before transit. Decryption keys stay within your link fragment and never touch our database servers.

📖 How to Use Zephyr Transfer

Follow this sequential workflow to deliver your files:

STEP 01

Select Your Files or Folders

Drag and drop your footage, archives, or folder trees into the transfer hub. Zephyr maintains exact nested directory paths.

STEP 02

Choose Delivery Mode

Select Get link for instant shareable URLs, or Send email to dispatch secure download links to your client.

STEP 03

Configure Security & Lifespan

Set link expiry (from 1 hour to 30 days) and optionally enable Burn-on-Read to automatically destroy files after the initial download.

STEP 04

Add Deliverable Escrow

Toggle Paywall and enter your invoice amount. Recipients must settle payment before downloading original uncompressed files.

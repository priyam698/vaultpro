Zephyr Platform: A to Z Breakdown

Complete overview of capabilities, security protocols, and vault limits.

A

Any File Type Storage

Acts like your native File Explorer. Store applications (.exe, .msi), disk images (.iso), packages (.apk), games, browser extensions, and archives without restriction.

C

3 TB Storage Capacity Ceiling

Stack subscription tiers up to a hard ceiling of 3 TB (3,000 GB). When an account reaches 3 TB, checkout requests are paused and on-screen alerts inform the user that capacity is capped.

E

End-to-End Zero Knowledge

All files are encrypted client-side using military-grade AES-256 before upload. Zephyr never stores your private decryption passphrases or unencrypted content.

M

Monetize Deliverables (Paywalls)

Set custom prices on client deliverables. Buyers can preview low-resolution protected media in our anti-screenshot viewer and unlock original downloads after checkout via Stripe.

G

5-Day Grace Period Safety

Cancelling auto-renew will never purge files immediately. Users receive an explicit 5-day grace period post-expiration to renew or safely export excess data before cleanup occurs.

S

Streaming & Edge Replication

Powered by Cloudflare R2 edge storage. Instant byte-range seeking enables direct playback of 4K/8K video without waiting for multi-gigabyte downloads to complete.

How to Install Zephyr on Your Devices

Windows, Mac & Linux Desktop
  1. Open Zephyr Drive in Chrome, Edge, or Brave.
  2. Click the "Install App" button above (or the screen icon 🖥️ in your browser address bar).
  3. Click Install to pin Zephyr directly to your Taskbar or Desktop.
iOS & Android Mobile
  1. Open this URL in mobile Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android).
  2. On iOS: Tap the Share button → tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. On Android: Tap the 3 dots menu → tap "Install app".

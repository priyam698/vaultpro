Legal Framework

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

Privora operates strictly on zero-knowledge encryption principles. When you protect an upload with a passcode, encryption keys are derived client-side within your browser. Privora does not store, transmit, or retain plaintext passcodes or file decryption keys. We cannot decrypt or inspect file payloads stored within our infrastructure.

2. Information We Collect

We minimize data collection to the operational requirements necessary to route encrypted blobs:

  • Guest Users: Anonymous transfer tokens, temporary browser hashes for abuse throttling, file size metrics, and expiration timestamps.
  • Registered & Pro Users: Account email addresses handled via Supabase Authentication and subscription tier IDs.
  • Telemetry: Temporary download counters and transfer completion statuses to trigger cryptographic shredding.

3. Data Retention & Cryptographic Shredding

Files uploaded to Privora are stored ephemerally across Cloudflare R2 edge locations. Objects are configured with strict automated expiration routines. Once an object exceeds its designated retention window (1h, 24h, 72h, or selected Pro intervals) or completes its single-download "Burn-on-Read" allocation, the object reference and underlying storage blocks are permanently purged with zero recovery capability.

4. Third-Party Infrastructure Sub-processors

Privora engages vetted edge infrastructure partners under strict confidentiality protocols:

  • Cloudflare R2: Encrypted edge object storage and CDN delivery.
  • Supabase: Encrypted user authentication and session management.
  • Render: API compute hosting and routing execution.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Privora does not employ third-party behavioral trackers, marketing analytics pixels, or advertising cookies. Browser localStorage is used solely to store session tokens and the complimentary guest transfer counter.

6. Your Rights

In accordance with GDPR and CCPA standards, users retain the right to delete their registered account and immediately cancel active shares from their dashboard at any time.

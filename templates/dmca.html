DMCA & Intellectual Property Compliance

Last updated: October 2026

1. Commitment to Intellectual Property

Zephyr Systems Inc. ("Zephyr") respects the intellectual property rights of creators and copyright holders. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512), Zephyr maintains an expedited process to address valid notices of claimed copyright infringement occurring on our ephemeral transfer pipelines.

2. Ephemeral Storage Architecture Notice

Zephyr operates as an automated transient transmission network and ephemeral cloud conduit:

  • Transfers are temporary by default, expiring automatically within 1 to 72 hours.
  • Burn-on-Read files are permanently shredded from Cloudflare R2 storage within 60 seconds of initial download.
  • Zephyr does not maintain a public search index, catalog, or public media directory.

3. Submitting a Takedown Notice

To file a legally sufficient copyright notice with Zephyr, you must provide written communication containing the following statutory details:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of the copyrighted work.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed.
  3. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing, specifically including the complete Zephyr transfer URL (e.g., https://zephyr-drive.onrender.com/share/xxxxxx).
  4. Your direct contact details (including full legal name, physical address, telephone number, and email address).
  5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
